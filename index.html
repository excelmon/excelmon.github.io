ABOUT

I'm driven by a simple question: why does this work the way it does? I like breaking complex systems down to first principles, setting aside assumptions until only the fundamentals are left, and then rebuilding from there. It's a skill I'm still sharpening, and one I expect to keep learning for a long time. In practice, most of what I build starts with a manual task that shouldn't be manual. My goal is to take that effort off people's plates and make the workday a little better for my teammates and for me.

My interests may appear unrelated at first glance. Software automation, process design, remote viewing, anomalous cognition, and consciousness research seem to exist in completely separate domains. To me, however, they are connected through a shared interest in systems, signal extraction, observation, and the limits of current models. Whether debugging software, analyzing workflows, or exploring claims surrounding consciousness and perception, I'm interested in how humans detect patterns, process information, and interact with complex systems.

Beyond software development, I enjoy exploring frontier topics related to consciousness, perception, and human potential. I follow scientific research and public discourse surrounding areas such as remote viewing, mind-matter interactions, extrasensory perception (ESP), and non-human intelligence (NHI). While many of these subjects remain controversial or poorly understood, I believe they raise important questions about the nature of consciousness and the boundaries of human experience.

We are living through a period of rapid technological and cultural change. From advances in artificial intelligence to growing public discussions surrounding anomalous phenomena and consciousness research, I find this era deeply inspiring. It motivates me to continue building, learning, questioning assumptions, and remaining adaptable in the face of emerging ideas and new discoveries.
